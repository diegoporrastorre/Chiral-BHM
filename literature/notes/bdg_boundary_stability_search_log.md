Date: 4 November 2026. Companion review: [Boundary dependent stability literature review](bdg_boundary_stability_literature_review.md).

## Search scope

The search used the available general web search service to locate primary arXiv manuscripts, APS papers, Quantum articles, Nature articles, author repositories, and a public patent disclosure. It included historical papers and records available through the search date. It was not a complete export of a specialist citation database, and inaccessible or unindexed work can remain missing.

## Full text and version limitations

Relevant full-text sections were consulted, not necessarily every page of every article. The companion review marks abstract-level screening separately. Source links identify the versions used.

The local Rassaert PDF contains the published main paper only. The publisher supplement URL could not be accessed by the web tool, and the publisher article interface reported supplemental access restrictions. The available arXiv v1 includes supplementary sections; III and VI were inspected for time evolution and the SVD construction. No claim is made that the final supplement was read or that it is identical to that preprint.

The file Longhi2021.pdf was identified by its title and arXiv number as the 2013 convective/absolute PT-breaking paper. Its internal later date is not the publication date. Original PDF names were preserved.

The local PDF extractor required approved access to its MiKTeX configuration. Extraction then succeeded; intermediate text is under tmp/ and is not a research deliverable.

## Limits of the novelty assessment

An exact matching V-dependent phase diagram was not located. That finding is narrower than saying no one has studied cross-Kerr BdG topology or boundary stabilization: both have direct precedents. Likewise, the exact recurrence and asymptotic thresholds in current project notes were not identified in the inspected passages, but this does not establish priority for those formulas.

No broad theorem about all topological amplifiers, all absorbing boundaries, or all nonlinear stationary branches is inferred from a single model or a patent. The companion review makes model-specific claims and distinguishes existing project results from literature results.
